# StudyMate V2 Roadmap

1. **Phase 1 — Architecture and migration:** additive schema, compatibility, service boundaries, deterministic calculations, tests and documentation.
2. **Phase 2 — Study Planner:** manual deterministic plans, scheduling workflows and validation.
3. **Phase 3 — Exam Preparation:** exam dashboards, topic progress and task integration.
4. **Phase 4 — Focus Sessions:** timer lifecycle, recovery, notifications and session history.
5. **Phase 5 — Advanced Analytics and Grade Predictor:** real-data trends, visualizations and what-if workflows.
6. **Phase 6 — Enhanced Notes/Documents:** richer organization, linking and search.
7. **Phase 7 — Optional Accounts and Cloud Sync:** explicit consent, authentication, stable sync identity and conflict handling.
8. **Phase 8 — StudyMate AI:** opt-in provider integration with transparent data boundaries and non-AI fallbacks.
9. **Phase 9 — Widgets and Smart Notifications:** platform widgets and user-controlled scheduling.
10. **Phase 10 — Final V2 QA/Release:** migration matrix, accessibility, performance, security, store assets and staged rollout.

Each phase must preserve offline access to saved academic data. Cloud and AI remain optional enhancements, never prerequisites.
