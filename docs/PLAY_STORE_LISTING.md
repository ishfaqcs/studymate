## Full description

StudyMate is a local-first academic planner for organizing the practical details of a semester.

### Plan your semester

Keep courses, weekly classes, rooms and academic schedules together.

### Track attendance

Record class attendance, compare it with course requirements, and understand safe misses or the recovery needed.

### Stay ahead of deadlines

Organize assignments, quizzes and exams with priorities, due dates and optional local reminders.

### Monitor grades and GPA

Record assessments, use a configurable grading scale, and review semester GPA, CGPA and academic history.

### Organize notes and documents

Keep searchable notes and app-managed copies of selected academic documents linked to courses.

### Keep data in your control

StudyMate V1 has no accounts, ads, analytics or cloud synchronization. Create validated local backups and a personal PDF academic summary when needed.
