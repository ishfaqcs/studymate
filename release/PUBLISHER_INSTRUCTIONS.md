# Publisher instructions

1. Create or open the StudyMate app entry in Play Console.
2. Confirm package `com.studymate.studentplanner`; it cannot be changed after upload.
3. Enable or confirm Play App Signing.
4. Complete the store listing and add real screenshots and a feature graphic.
5. Supply the real developer support email.
6. Host the privacy policy publicly and add its HTTPS URL.
7. Complete Data Safety.
8. Complete Content Rating.
9. Complete Target Audience.
10. Complete the Ads declaration.
11. Complete App Access.
12. Create an Internal testing release first.
13. Upload `StudyMate-1.0.0.aab` only after its signature, package, version, and contents have been verified.
14. Resolve all Play Console errors and evaluate every warning.
15. Install StudyMate from the Play internal-test link.
16. Perform final QA of startup, core records, documents, backup/restore, notifications, and dark mode.
17. Move to production only after Play-distributed QA passes and listing/privacy information is final.

Do not place Play Console credentials, signing passwords, `key.properties`, or a keystore in this folder.
